How endpoint check-ins work

The menu-bar client runs at launch, on demand, and every 24 hours while open. Report recency uses a 36-hour window. Updated clients check in separately every five minutes while open; after 15 minutes without a client check-in, presence is overdue. Older clients have unknown presence.

Profiles & device setup

macOS 26 Tahoe profiles: Level 1 and Level 2 are based on the pinned NIST mSCP Tahoe Revision 3 baselines for CIS macOS 26.0 Benchmark v1.1.0. The CSP client runs only its bundled read-only mappings; unsupported checks stay manual. FileVault results that pass the boot-volume check are recorded as manual until connected drives and volumes are checked. Preference results describe the reporting user’s effective settings, while system checks describe the device; they do not prove enforcement for every user. The displayed percentage is a pass rate across all checks, not a CIS attestation. Source attribution and license are included with each profile.
