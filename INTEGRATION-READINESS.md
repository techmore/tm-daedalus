## Linux scanner removal and recovery

Release 049 adds `manage-service-linux.sh uninstall|restore`. Removal verifies the original private units/environment and the manager's loaded fragment path, refuses drop-ins, stops/disables the bridge before NmapUI, and retains enrollment, scanner evidence, runtime files, and exact recovery descriptors. Restore reinstates those descriptors and checks that both services are enabled and active. A private lock rejects concurrent uninstall/restore calls. Failures retain the recovery files for retry. This is an explicit local lifecycle command; portal enrollment is not revoked.
